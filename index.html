Official Rules

1. Eligibility. You must be 18 or older. One account per person, tied to one email address.

2. The Weekly Contest. The Weekly Contest is held once a week, every Tuesday at 9:00 PM Eastern Time (ET), and is open for one hour (9:00 to 10:00 PM ET). You must be signed in with your email to play it.

3. Prizes are paid only during the Weekly Contest. The total prize pot is $20 per week. Based on each player's best score in that contest: 1st place wins $5, 2nd place $3, 3rd place $2, and places 4 through 25 share the remaining $10 equally. If fewer than 25 players score, the prizes are adjusted so 1st, 2nd and 3rd always win the most: no place from 4th down wins more than $1.00, and the rest of the $20 pot is added to the top three in a 50/30/20 split. With only 1 to 3 players, those places win $5, $3 and $2. Ties are broken by whoever reached the score first. Nothing is paid for play outside the contest.

4. Playing outside the contest. You may play at any other time. If you save your result with your email, you earn extra play entries for the Weekly Contest: Knight (6+ correct) 1 entry, Duke (8+ correct) 3 entries, Crowned (10 of 10) 10 entries. Each account can earn entries from one play per day (Eastern Time). Entries stay in your account until used.

5. Using extra play entries. Your first play in a Weekly Contest is free. Each extra play entry lets you play the contest one more time, using one entry. Your best score in that contest counts.

6. Timing. Each question allows 10 seconds. An unanswered question counts as wrong. Faster correct answers score more points.

7. Winnings and cash out. Prizes are added to your account after the contest closes. Winnings stack from week to week until you cash out. To cash out, sign in with your email and enter your Cash App $cashtag or Venmo username. Payouts are sent through Cash App or Venmo to the account you provide; you are responsible for entering it correctly. Entries have no cash value and cannot be sold or transferred.

8. Fair play. Cheating, multiple accounts, or automated play will result in disqualification and forfeiture of entries and winnings. Answer times are measured by our server. Prizes and cash-outs are subject to review, and we may withhold or void any prize we reasonably believe was won unfairly. A payout account (Cash App or Venmo) can be linked to only one player account.
